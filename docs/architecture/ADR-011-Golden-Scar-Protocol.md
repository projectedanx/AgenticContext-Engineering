# Pluriversal_Knowledge_Capsule

### Architectural Topology Shift
The introduction of `GoldenScarProtocol.tsx` creates a deliberate bottleneck in the UI where humans MUST define the tension (Constraint A vs. Constraint B) rather than asking the AI to "solve the problem." The AI's job is to calculate the **interference fit** (the exact force/structure needed to hold the two conflicting concepts together without destroying either).

This is the physical manifestation of **Epsilon-Tolerance Paraconsistency**. Technical debt is no longer tracked as a failure state, but as a known topological deformation that the overarching workflow is currently capable of sustaining (a transition fit).

### Execution Context
The component exposes two primary operations:
1.  **Topological Derivative Calculator:** For resolving human-driven stakeholder dissonance.
2.  **Epsilon-Tolerance Tech Debt Evaluator:** For structurally logging tech debt as a stable Transition Fit rather than an error.
